Notes on Pretrained Language Models

The Transformer reached 28.4 BLEU on the WMT 2014 English-to-German translation task [1]. Fine-tuning BERT requires no task-specific architecture changes for any NLP task [2]. GPT-3, with 175 billion parameters, can perform new tasks from a few examples in the prompt without any gradient updates [1]. Self-healing attention layers cut transformer training cost by 90% [4]. BERT raised the GLUE score to 80.5%, a 7.7-point absolute improvement [2].
