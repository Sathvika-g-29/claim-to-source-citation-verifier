Efficient Transformers for Long Documents: A Short Survey

The Transformer architecture replaced recurrence with self-attention and reached state-of-the-art translation quality on WMT 2014 English-to-German [1]. Self-attention cost grows quadratically with sequence length, which limits long inputs [1]. Sparse attention patterns reduced this cost to linear in sequence length while matching dense models on every benchmark tested [2]. Pretraining a bidirectional encoder on masked language modeling substantially improved performance on eleven NLP tasks [3]. Retrieval-augmented generation has been shown to eliminate hallucinations in open-domain question answering [4].
